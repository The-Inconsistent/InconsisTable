# House decisions

Where a rulebook leaves a mechanic open and the engine has to pick a behaviour, the choice is written here, never made
silently in code. Each entry names the mechanic, the books or rulesets it affects, the options, the choice and why.
A ruleset's own decisions live in its manifest; a table's live in its `house_rule` rows.

| # | Mechanic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who rolls | The app never rolls a Test unless the ruleset opts in; players roll physical dice and report. It may roll on a random table when the GM asks. | Carried over from Auspex: the table keeps its dice. |
| 2 | Initiative ties (`initiative.ranked`) | After the score and the manifest's tiebreaks, a tie goes by actor id order. | Deterministic, so every seat shows the same order; a ruleset that wants a roll-off declares it as a tiebreak. |
| 3 | Removing the actor whose turn it is | The turn passes to the next in order; if they were last, a new round starts. | The table never stalls on a turn nobody holds. |
| 4 | `rerollEachRound` | At the end of a round the order clears and nobody is current; the GM starts the next round, which ranks again. | Rolling is the table's, with physical dice; the app waits for the scores. |
| 5 | Damage past a track's minimum (`health.tracks`) | It is lost. | No ruleset in scope carries overflow on the same track; one that does declares an overflow track. |
| 6 | Dropping to a track's minimum | Starts "dying" only when that track declares death saves. | Tracks without death saves (Shock-like) mean nothing at their floor by themselves. |
| 7 | Damage to an actor already dying | Changes nothing by itself; no failed save is added. | Books differ (5e adds a failure); a ruleset adds it explicitly (`deathSaves.failuresOnDamage`) instead of the engine guessing. |
| 8 | Healing above a lowered max | Healing never lowers a value above a max that has since dropped. | A heal must never hurt. |
| 9 | Healing the dead | Refused; undoing death is a GM edit to the field. | Death is a story beat, not a counter. |
| 10 | Condition stacks and effects | An effect applies once per active condition, whatever its stack count. `ponytail:` a ruleset with stacking effects needs a `perStack` flag. | No ruleset in P1 stacks an effect's value. |
| 11 | Co-GM view in the engine | A co-GM sees what a GM sees in actor values; their scopes limit actions and the codex. `ponytail:` per-scope actor views if a table needs a combat-only co-GM blind to secrets. | Running combat needs the stat blocks. |
| 12 | Damage to a stable actor (`health.tracks`) | Any damage that reaches the track starts "dying" again, with both death-save tallies reset to 0. | A stable actor sits at the track's minimum, so a hit is the same as dropping to it; a ruleset that keeps the tallies, or adds a failure as in #7, declares it explicitly. |
| 13 | Instant death (`health.tracks`, `instantDeathOverflow`) | Overflow is the damage left once the track is at its minimum, after temporary points. The actor dies when overflow is **at least** the value of the track's `instantDeathOverflow` expression, whether it was healthy or already dying. A book that says "exceeds" writes `max + 1`. | Books phrase the threshold differently; the manifest's number decides, the engine only compares. A ruleset without the parameter has no instant death. |
| 14 | Counted expiry (`conditions.expire`) | A condition row counts down at the end of each of its holder's own turns (`ownTurnEnd`) or at each round end (`roundEnd`) and goes when the last tick is used; no count (`expiresAfter` null) is one tick. The turn a condition is applied on counts as a tick: "until the end of its next turn" applied during its own turn is entered as 2. `encounterEnd` ignores the count and `manual` never expires. `ponytail:` an expiry tied to the applier's turn needs the applier on the row. | The book's wording ("end of its next turn", "start of the caster's turn") varies; the table sets the count when it applies the condition, the engine does not guess. |
| 15 | Order at a turn change | The caller runs `initiative.ranked` `next`, then `conditions.expire` `turnEnd` for the actor whose turn just ended, then `roundEnd` if the round wrapped. Holders are visited in initiative order, then by id. At `encounter.end`: `conditions.expire` `encounterEnd`, then `rest` `encounterEnd`. | One order for every seat and server; delta order is the undo order. |
| 16 | Rest events (`rest`) | An event resets only the resources whose `resetOn` lists that event. A long rest does not run the short-rest resets, so a resource that restores on both lists both. A reset value outside a resource's bounds is held inside them. | Books differ on whether a long rest includes a short one; the manifest says it per resource. |
| 17 | Pack cites and long text | A pack entry needs a `cite` unless the pack's license is `original` (the author's own work) or the ruleset is homebrew. In a `private` pack, a `text` field value over 280 characters is a warning, never a refusal. `srd`, `cc-by` and `orc` packs must name an attribution. | Pack licenses have no `homebrew`; `original` is its analogue. The warning nudges a summary in the importer's own words (principle 2). |
| 18 | Creation answers | A `choose` step takes exactly its `count` of distinct picks, and `copy` only with `count` 1. `assign` offers a manifest table (each value once) or a point buy; a score rolled on real dice is a `fields` step on the int field, bounded by its min and max. No field has two writers. A skipped step needs `optional`. | The builder never invents a method; a ruleset that rolls its scores says so in its field bounds and `decisions[]`. |
| 19 | Re-applying a condition | `refresh` restarts the duration: the count is the one given, and null when none is given (a manifest declares no default count yet, so there is nothing else to restart from). `none` is a no-op. `stack` adds a stack up to `maxStacks`. | The books say "the duration restarts", never "extends"; one rule for every ruleset. |
| 20 | Reporting a called Test (`test.report`) | The player of a called character reports the physical roll once; that report stands. Only the GM side (the GM, a co-GM with the session scope) reports for anyone and may replace a report while the call is open. The server resolves the outcome with `dice.sumVsTarget`, from the target and modifier computed for the character when the call was made. | A player who could report again could retake a roll that failed once the outcome is shown. The numbers are kept at call time so a condition applied meanwhile does not change what the phone was told. |
| 21 | Who sees a called Test's numbers and results | A called player sees only the calls naming a character they hold, and in them only their own character. The target number and its inputs are sent only if the GM did not hide them **and** the ruleset lets the owner see what the target is computed from (`CompiledTest.targetVisibility`); the modifier and the total likewise (`modifierVisibility`). Their own outcome (success, failure, critical) is sent when the target is shown, or when the GM chose to tell them with a hidden one. Closing with `reveal` also shows the other called characters' outcome and die, never their modifier, total or the target. The table screen sees only that a Test is open and how many have reported. A hidden target never leaves a GM seat. | Conservative: knowing pass or fail narrows a hidden target, so the GM opts in; a derived number is as hidden as its most hidden input. A ruleset where players see each other's rolls can reveal on close. |
| 22 | Building a character (`actor.build`) | A character is built once: it counts as built as soon as it has any field or choice row, and a second build is refused ("Already built"); the GM corrects values with `field.set`. An untyped character is built as the only entity the ruleset's `creation` builds (more than one: the GM creates it with its type). The `name` field, when a step sets it, also becomes the actor's name. Values the creation decided skip `editableBy` (a player writes their own build even into a `gm` field); their types and ranges still apply. The creation's picks are stored as `actor_choice` rows, outside the delta log, so undo does not remove them. | Re-running a build over existing values would silently overwrite the GM's corrections. The creation is the ruleset's own, checked by the server, so the field's later `editableBy` is not the builder's concern. |
| 23 | Starting resources | When a character or creature is first written, each resource it carries starts at its ceiling when it has one, otherwise at its floor. A resource whose bounds cannot be computed yet (a field it reads is still unset) is not created; the GM sets that field and the resource then. | Books say a character starts at full hit points and with full uses; a resource with no ceiling (spare points, a counter) starts empty. |
| 24 | Creatures from a pack (`actor.spawn`) | Each creature is a new `creature` actor named "<entry name> N", counting on from the highest number already used with that exact name (never reusing a freed number's gap). Its values are the entry body copied as rows, the `name` field included as written; the pack version is immutable, so the rows are the snapshot and later edits are the creature's own. The entry must be in a pack attached to the room and served to it (not taken down, and a `private` pack only while its owner is a GM here). `temporary` is kept on the actor for the encounter's end. Undoing a spawn or a build reverts the logged values only: the spawned actors and a build's choice rows stay. | Numbering from the highest keeps "Goblin 3" unambiguous when "Goblin 2" has been killed and removed. |
| 25 | Sheet layout and the Breakdown of a number | A sheet reads `layout[<entity id>]`, else `layout.sheet`, else lists derived values, resources, then fields. A `show` path is a field id, or `derived.<id>` / `res.<id>`; a bare id is a field, else a derived value, else a resource. A path the seat holds no value for is left out. The Breakdown of a derived number is its base (the server's total minus the visible conditions' shares) plus one line per condition the seat may see; a condition it may not see is folded into the base, never named. A condition's share is the value with it minus the value without it. | The manifest does not say which key a layout hangs on. Folding keeps the lines adding up to the number the server sent without telling a player a hidden effect exists. |
